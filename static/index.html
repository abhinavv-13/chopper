Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Chopper is built from the ground up on an on-device privacy architecture. We believe creators should maintain full ownership and confidentiality over their raw footage, audio streams, and generated clips.

1. Zero Cloud Media Storage

All video decoding, facecam tracking, audio spike detection, speech transcription, and 1080p rendering run 100% locally on your machine. Your raw stream footage and rendered clips are never transmitted to external cloud servers or third-party AI training pipelines.

2. Local Model Execution

Whisper AI speech recognition and OpenCV computer vision models execute in your local runtime. Transcripts and word alignments never leave your device.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Temporary processing files are stored in your local application directory and can be purged at any time. Chopper does not track your viewing habits or resell creator analytics.
